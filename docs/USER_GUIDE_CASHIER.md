# Cashier guide — Paper & Pixels

Everything you do at the counter, step by step. Kapag may mali, **huwag mag-delete** — cancel or void with a reason (see the last section).

## 1. Log in
Open the app on the shop PC → enter your **username** (lowercase) and password → **Log in**.
First time? Go to **My account → Change password** right away.

## 2. Find or add the customer
**Customers** → search by name, mobile or Facebook name. Not there? **+ New customer** (name is required; add mobile `09xx…` and Facebook name so you can message them). You can also add a customer directly on the New Order screen.
The customer page shows their **balance**, orders and payments.

## 3. New order
1. **+ New Order** → pick the customer.
2. Add each product. Choose the options (cover type, size, add-ons…); for tarpaulin enter **width and height in feet**; for albums enter the **number of leaves**. The price is computed automatically.
3. **Price override** (e.g. suki price): change the unit price and type the **reason** — required.
4. **Specs**: write exactly what the customer wants (names, dates, colors, layout). Pick the **file source** (Gmail, USB, Messenger) and add a note like "Drive link sent 10/16".
5. Set the **target date** (when the customer will claim).
6. **Discount** on the whole order? Enter the amount and the reason, then ask the **owner to enter their approval PIN** to approve it. Without it the order will not save.

## 4. Take payment
Choose the payment terms:
- **Full payment** — pays everything now.
- **Downpayment** — usually 50%; enter the amount received.
- **Pay after production** — no payment yet, pays on pickup.
- **Credit** — for trusted customers only; balance stays on their account.

Pick the method (**Cash, GCash, Maya, Bank transfer**). For GCash / Maya / bank, also pick **Received in** — which of the shop's accounts the customer sent the money to (check the customer's screenshot) — and type the **reference number**. Save. The order gets a number like **PP-2026-00012** and each payment a receipt number like **AR-2026-00034**.

## 5. Print the order slip
On the order page click **Print order slip** → give the customer their copy. Ask them to bring it on pickup.

## 6. Add a later payment / print a receipt
Open the order (Orders → search the order no. or customer) → **Add payment** → amount + method + reference → Save. Click **Print receipt** next to the payment. Partial payments are fine; the balance updates automatically.

## 7. Release / deliver the order
When the order is **Completed** and the **balance is ₱0**, click **Mark delivered / picked up**. If there's still a balance, the app will not let you — collect first, or ask the owner (admin) to override with a reason.

## 8. Deliveries
For Lalamove/Grab, LBC/J&T or own delivery: **Deliveries → + New delivery** → choose the order, method, address, recipient, contact no., rider/tracking no., fee. The fee is just for the record — it is **not added** to the order total. Update the status (In transit → Delivered) and print the delivery slip if needed.

## 9. Assign workers (Production Board)
**Production Board** shows a card for every job. Open a card (or use the dropdown) to **assign a worker** — a job cannot start until someone is assigned. Red cards are **overdue**. You can move cards between Pending, In Process, Quality Check and Completed.

## 10. Record expenses
**Expenses → + New expense** → date, category (Materials, Utilities, Transport, Meals…), amount, paid via, notes, and a **photo of the receipt** if you have one.

## 11. Purchase orders & receiving stock
- **Purchase Orders → + New PO** → supplier, materials, quantities and unit cost → save/print and send to the supplier.
- When the delivery arrives: open the PO → **Receive** → enter what actually arrived (partial is OK). Stock goes up automatically.
- Paid the supplier? Record it on the supplier page (**Payments**).

## 12. Low stock
**Inventory** highlights materials at or below their reorder level (also on the Dashboard). Tell the owner or create a PO. Materials are deducted automatically when jobs start, so keep the stock counts honest — use **Adjust stock** after a physical count (with a note).

## 13. Made a mistake? Never delete.
- **Wrong order / customer backed out** → open the order → **Cancel order** → type the reason.
- **Wrong payment entered** → on the order, click **Void** next to the payment, type the reason, and have the **owner enter their approval PIN** → **Yes, continue**. Then enter the correct payment.
- **Wrong expense** → ask the owner to **Void** it with a reason.
- **Wrong customer details** → just edit and save.

Everything is recorded in the audit log, so always write a clear reason. Hindi sigurado? Ask the owner before saving.
