# Paper & Pixels — Inventory Personnel Guide (1 page)

You manage the stockroom: you take materials out for production, add stock that arrives, and keep counts right.
Log in with your own username. You will only see the **Inventory** menu.

## 1. Stock out (releasing materials) — the main job
1. Open **Stock Out** (first item in the menu).
2. **Material** — type a few letters in *Search material…* to narrow the list, then choose it. The box below shows how much is **on hand**.
3. **Quantity out** — in the material's unit (sheet, pc, ml, sq ft…). You cannot take out more than is on hand.
4. **Released to (worker)** — choose the production worker who is receiving it (e.g. Juan, Maria).
5. **Notes / purpose** — optional when released to a worker (e.g. "for PP-2026-00012 album covers").
   If it is *not* going to a worker (damaged, sample, waste), leave *Released to* blank and **write the reason** — it is required.
6. Tap **Take out of stock**. The stock is deducted immediately and the line appears under **Today's stock-outs**.

> Materials used by jobs on the Production Board are deducted **automatically** when a job starts.
> Use Stock Out for anything extra: re-dos, replacements, samples, damaged items, or materials handed out in bulk.

## 2. Stock in (materials arrived without a PO)
Open **Materials** → the material → **Stock in**: quantity and the unit cost you paid. (Deliveries with a purchase order are received by staff on the PO page.)

## 3. Physical count
Once a month (or when something looks wrong): **Materials** → the material → **Adjustment** → enter the quantity you actually counted and the reason. The system records the difference.

## 4. Cutting / conversion
When you cut a big sheet into pieces (e.g. 1 full PVC 4x8 → 24 pcs 12x12): **Cutting / Conversion** → input material and qty → output material and qty → waste. Both stocks update together.

## 5. Checking history
**Stock Movements** shows every in/out with date, quantity, who did it and who received it. Filter by material, type (e.g. *Stock out*) or date.

## Rules
- Never "fix" stock by taking out or adding random amounts — use **Adjustment** with a reason.
- Red / "low stock" means it is at or below the reorder level — tell the owner or cashier so they can order.
- Nothing can be deleted; every movement is recorded with your name.
