# Production worker guide — Paper & Pixels

## 1. Log in on your phone
Open the shop's app link in Chrome/Safari → enter your **username** and password → **Log in**.
Tip: tap the browser menu → **Add to Home screen** so it opens like an app.
First time? **My account → Change password**.

## 2. My Jobs
After logging in you see **My Jobs** — only the jobs assigned to you, grouped by status:
**Pending → In Process → Quality Check → Completed**.
A **red** card or date means the job is **overdue** — do these first.

## 3. Open a job and read the specs
Tap a job card to see:
- the order number, customer name and **due date**
- the product and every option chosen (size, cover, number of leaves, add-ons…)
- the **specs notes** (names, dates, colors, layout) and where the files are (Gmail, USB, Messenger)

Read everything **before** you start cutting or printing.

## 4. Start the job
Tap **Start (In Process)**. You can only start jobs assigned to you. Jobs nobody has taken yet show **Worker: Unassigned — available** with a **✋ Take this job** button: tap it and the job becomes yours, then press Start. If a job is assigned to someone else it won't be on your list — ask the cashier.
When you start, the materials for the job are deducted from inventory automatically.

## 5. Add notes and photos
Use **Add note / photo** to record progress: "Layout done", "Waiting for glass", or a photo of the finished piece. The cashier and owner see these right away — useful when the customer asks for an update.

## 6. Finish
- Done making it? Move it to **Quality Check** so it can be checked (spelling, colors, cut, glass clean).
- Checked and ready for the customer? Move it to **Completed**. When all items of the order are completed, the order shows as ready for pickup.

## 7. Something missing or unclear?
Wrong size, missing file, blurry photo, not enough material, names not sure? **Stop and ask the staff/cashier** before continuing, and add a note on the job explaining the problem. Better to ask than to redo the work.

## 8. Time in and time out (Time Clock)
1. On the shop PC (or your phone), open **Time Clock**.
2. Tap **your name**. The camera turns on — **look at the camera**, type your **4-digit PIN**, press **Enter**. Your photo is taken at that moment and you'll see a big green **TIME IN** with the time and your photo.
3. At the end of the day do the same — it shows **TIME OUT**.
- Keep your PIN secret; never time in for someone else — every punch is recorded **with your photo**.
- First time on a device: the browser asks to use the camera — tap **Allow**.
- Forgot to time in/out? Tell the owner — they can correct it.
- If your login is linked to your employee record, **My Time & Pay** shows your own time records and payslips.
